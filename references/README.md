# Reference corpus

The three books in `OneDrive - Zivid AS/Documents/Books/optics` are the primary references. Fetch them via [this link](https://shapecrafter-my.sharepoint.com/:f:/g/personal/otto_hanski_zivid_com/IgDPLFGBiq-1QYTKoVnMdCZxAezQ6Ghh8WKQqH9YBQDjbKs?e=FSANjF). Lessons cite them by tag, section and **printed** page number (not PDF page).

| Tag | Book | Role in the course | PDF page = printed page + |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Geary]** | Joseph M. Geary, *Introduction to Lens Design: With Practical ZEMAX Examples*, Willmann-Bell, 2002 | Main lens-design textbook. Lecture-based, with ZEMAX homework. Its menu names come from ZEMAX SE v6–7 (2002); the lessons translate them to the current OpticStudio ribbon UI. | 17 |
| **[Kidger]** | Michael J. Kidger, *Fundamental Optical Design*, SPIE Press (PM92), 2002 | Theory: paraxial optics, ray tracing, Seidel aberrations, classical design forms. | 19 |
| **[Kenyon]** | Ian R. Kenyon, *The Light Fantastic: A Modern Introduction to Classical and Quantum Optics*, Oxford University Press, 2008 | Physical optics: waves, interference, diffraction, Fourier optics, polarization, lasers, detectors, radiometry. | 23 |

Example: `[Kidger §6.2.1, pp. 102–107]` is printed page 102, which is PDF page 121.

## Recommended further reading (not in the corpus)

- W. J. Smith, *Modern Optical Engineering*: the standard practical handbook.
- R. Kingslake & R. B. Johnson, *Lens Design Fundamentals*.
- R. E. Fischer, B. Tadic-Galeb, P. R. Yoder, *Optical System Design*: strong on tolerancing and practical engineering.
- H. Gross et al., *Handbook of Optical Systems* (Wiley), and Herbert Gross's lens-design lecture slides from Jena.
- Ansys Zemax OpticStudio Help, available in-program as F1 or Help → Help PDF.
